QUESTIONS

1. IDENTIFIER WITHOUT A BARCODE
   The required "identifier" field — must it be a GTIN/EAN, or will you accept
   an internal product code? Our products are sold by the slice from a display
   case and carry no barcode. Our internal codes look like
   NUTRIKO-TORTA-TIRAMISU.

2. SERVING = A SLICE OF A WHOLE CAKE
   Can the standard serving be a slice rather than a package — for example
   183 g out of a 2196 g cake? We know the exact slice weight and the number of
   slices per cake for every product.

3. POLYOLS
   Our products are sweetened with erythritol. Does your schema have a separate
   sugar alcohol / polyol field alongside carbohydrate? We would like to declare
   total carbohydrate with polyols shown separately, consistent with EU
   labelling, rather than net carbohydrate. For example, one slice of our
   tiramisu is 23.8 g carbohydrate, of which 17.5 g erythritol, leaving 6.3 g
   net.

4. BULGARIAN LANGUAGE AND MARKET
   Can product names and descriptions be submitted in Bulgarian (Cyrillic), and
   can we set Bulgaria as the geographic market?

5. WHERE THE DATA APPEARS
   Which consumer apps and partners would show our products once published?

We can send the full data set as a CSV if that helps you answer.
